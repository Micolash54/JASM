<p align="center"><b>Just Another Storage Mod</b>: portable, recoverable digital storage for Minecraft.</p>

---

> [!WARNING]
> JASM is still in **beta**. It works, but it's young: things can break, change between versions, or behave in ways I haven't caught yet. Found a bug? Open an issue and I'll take a look.


JASM stores your items as data on small chips called wafers. You carry them in a handheld Deck, so your storage goes where you go. And you back them up at an Archive, so a lost wafer can be rebuilt instead of gone for good.

Little robots called Bitlings do a lot of the work. Find one in the wild, befriend it with a chip, and it'll make chips for you, run your network's brain or knock down blocks with a laser.

## The guide

Everything is explained in the [JASM guide](https://micolash54.github.io/JASM/), with recipes and a page for every item. It's in the game too: craft a JASM Guide from a Book and a Data Crystal, hold G over any JASM item, or press "?" on a machine screen.

## A quick look

- **Wafers and Decks.** Wafers hold items or fluids, from a few stacks up to a million. A Deck reads several at once and you can search everything from one screen. [How storage works](https://micolash54.github.io/JASM/26.1/mechanics/how-storage-works.html)
- **Backups.** An Archive keeps a copy of your wafers and can rebuild one onto a blank chip if you lose it. [Backups and recovery](https://micolash54.github.io/JASM/26.1/mechanics/backups-and-recovery.html)
- **Autocrafting.** Write recipes onto cards and ask your Crafting Deck for anything; the parts get crafted first. Furnaces and modded machines join in through Access Ports, fluids included, and crafting rules keep your stock topped up. [Autocrafting](https://micolash54.github.io/JASM/26.1/mechanics/autocrafting.html)
- **Networks.** Cables carry power and link your machines. A network runs 4 machines on its own; past that it needs a Network Brain, and a tower of brain floors lets it grow much bigger. [Networks](https://micolash54.github.io/JASM/26.1/mechanics/networks.html)
- **Ports and Bays.** Input and Output Ports move things between chests and your Deck, and a Storage Port lets the whole network use a chest or tank. Bays place and break blocks for you.
- **Bitlings** grow from Basic Bitlings into Nibblings and Bytelings as they work, and the best wafers need what only a Byteling can make. [Befriending Bitlings](https://micolash54.github.io/JASM/26.1/mechanics/befriending-bitlings.html)
- **Playing with friends.** Everyone on a network gets their own Deck and their own stuff, shares the recipes, and can send items to each other. [Co-op play](https://micolash54.github.io/JASM/26.1/mechanics/co-op-play.html)

## Requirements

- Minecraft 26.1.2
- NeoForge 26.1.2.109 or newer
- [GuideME](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/guideme) 26.1.12-beta or newer (26.1.14 or newer if you want the guide's toolbar buttons to work)

Optional:

- [JEI](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/jei) 29.37.0.99 or newer: all recipes and info pages, recipe keys in the Deck's grid, and JEI's "+" fills the Crafting Deck and the Encoding Terminal.
- [Jade](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/jade) 26.1.10+neoforge or newer shows what's going on inside JASM blocks when you look at them.

## Installing

Download the latest jar from [Releases](https://github.com/Micolash54/JASM/releases) and put it in your `mods` folder, next to GuideME.

Updating an old world? Back it up first and read that release's notes, some versions change how things work.
