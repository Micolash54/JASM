Recipe Rack

26.1.2JASM 0.6.4

A Recipe Rack holds 16 filled Recipe Cards. Every Crafting Server on the same network can use them.

At a glance
Holds16 cards
Uses2 FE a tick
Counts towardthe machine limit

Without power, the network can't see the cards inside, so autocrafting stops. Jade shows how many cards a rack holds.

Need more than 16 recipes? Add another rack.
