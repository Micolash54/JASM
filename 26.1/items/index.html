Items, blocks and machines

26.1.2JASM 0.6.3

One page for each item, block and machine, with its recipe. Hold the guide key over an item to jump to its page. The pages are in the order you will meet them.

Crystals and chips

  • JASM Guide: the book you are reading.
  • Crystal Seed: turns amethyst into a crystal farm.
  • Seeded Amethyst: where Data Crystals grow.
  • Data Crystal: the raw material.
  • Crystal Dust: what a bud broken early leaves.
  • Crystal Resonator: makes crystals grow faster.
  • Crystal Foundry: grows crystals in bulk.
  • Blank Chip: cut from a Data Crystal.
  • Logic and Memory Chips: the parts of every recipe.

Bitlings and the Workshop

  • Chip Workshop: a Bitling turns Blank Chips into better ones.
  • Bitling Station: a home for your Bitling.
  • Bitlings: the helpers.
  • Nibblings and Bytelings: the grown-up stages.
  • Link Chip: for the third tier.
  • Advanced Chips: rare, for the top tier.
  • Synapse Core: unlocks the biggest wafers.

Storage

  • Capacity Wafers: any mix of items.
  • Type Wafers: a few kinds, in bulk.
  • Fluid Capacity Wafers: any mix of fluids.
  • Fluid Type Wafers: a few fluids, in bulk.
  • Decks: the handheld reader.
  • Crafting Decks: a Deck with a crafting grid.
  • Dimension Upgrade: use a Deck outside the Overworld.
  • Archives: backups for your wafers.

Network and power

  • Data Cables: join machines and carry power.
  • Power Acceptor: moves power between a network and other mods' blocks.
  • Combustion Generators: burn fuel for power.
  • Battery: stores power and joins up with its neighbours.
  • Network Brain: lifts the machine limit.
  • Network Chamber: builds brain floors and towers.

Ports and bays

  • Access Port: talks to other machines.
  • Item ports: move items and fluids in and out of a Deck.
  • Storage Port: lends a chest or tank to the network.
  • Speed Upgrade, Redstone Upgrade and Power Upgrade.
  • Crafting Upgrade and Stock Upgrade: for Output Ports.
  • Deployment Bay: places blocks and fluids.
  • Demolition Bay: breaks blocks.
  • Wrench: turn machines and pick them up.

Autocrafting

  • Encoding Terminal: writes recipes and pairs Decks.
  • Recipe Cards: hold one recipe each.
  • Recipe Rack: stores 16 cards.
  • Crafting Server: runs the jobs.
  • Advanced Crafting Server: the same with room for ten of everything.
  • Processors: how many crafts run at once.
  • Storage Modules: how big a job fits.
