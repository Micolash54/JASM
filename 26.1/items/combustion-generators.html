Combustion Generators

26.1.2JASM 0.6.1

Burn anything a furnace burns and get power. A generator sends it into every block it touches, and charges Decks and other chargeable items in its charging slot.

TierBurnsMakesHoldsGives per block
Basic2x faster40 FE a tick100,000 FE1,000 FE a tick
Advanced4x faster160 FE a tick400,000 FE2,500 FE a tick
Elite8x faster640 FE a tick1,000,000 FE5,000 FE a tick

A piece of coal gives 32,000 FE in a Basic generator over 40 seconds, 64,000 FE over 20 seconds in an Advanced one and 128,000 FE over 10 seconds in an Elite one.

Tips

  • Put one next to a Chip Workshop or an Archive to power it without any cable.
  • Drop a Deck in the charging slot to top up its battery.
  • Jade shows how long the fuel has left.

Upgrading

The old generator is used up. The new one keeps its stored power.
