Settings

26.1.2JASM 0.7.3

Every number and switch JASM lets you change, in the order the config screen shows them.

Changing a value

  • Choose Mods, JASM, Config. The sections are on the left and each setting has a short description.
  • The screen has two parts: My settings (jasm-client.toml) and World rules (jasm-server.toml).
  • My settings can always be changed. World rules can be changed in singleplayer and by operators on a server. Everyone else sees them locked, and with no world open they show the normal values.
  • A changed value turns yellow and gets a Reset key. Reset section puts the open section back to its defaults.
  • A value outside its range can't be saved. Changes are saved when you press Done.

When a change applies

  • Settings marked Needs restart (Deck slots, the features and the Basic Bitling recipe) apply after the world reloads, or after the server restarts. The rest apply at once.
  • Players who join a server get the server's world rules. A server's jasm-server.toml can also be edited by hand.

Where the values are kept

  • Each world keeps its own copy, at saves/<world>/serverconfig/jasm-server.toml.
  • The copy in config/ is only what new worlds start with.
  • An update never changes a value that is already saved in a file. An old world keeps its old numbers until you change them.

Speed Upgrades

Both lists go 0, 1, 2, 3 and 4 Speed Upgrades. More upgrades use the last entry. They apply to the Deployment and Demolition Bays, the Crystal Foundry and the Chip Workshop (chips and the Synapse Core). Ports have their own steps.

SettingKeyWhat it does
Speed by upgrades (times as fast)speedMultipliersList: how many times as fast a machine works. A job's time is its base time divided by this, half ticks rounded up, at least 1 tick.
Price by upgrades (times the base)priceMultipliersList: what one job costs, as a multiple of its base price.
