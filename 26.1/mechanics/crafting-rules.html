Crafting rules

26.1.2JASM 0.6.1

A rule makes the network craft for you, without you asking. Set them on the Rules tab of a Crafting Deck.

Two kinds

RuleExample
Fewer thanwhen I have fewer than 16 torches, craft 32
Everyevery minute, craft 8 bread

Pick the item by holding it and clicking. A timer can be no shorter than 10 seconds by default.

Where it goes

Choose where the results go: onto the Deck, or straight into your inventory. A "fewer than" rule counts what you carry when it sends to you.

Good to know

  • A rule runs one job at a time.
  • If a job can't start, the rule waits 5 seconds and tries again. The rule's line says why.
  • Each rule can be switched on or off.
  • The rules need the same chain as autocrafting: recipe cards, a rack, a server.
