Sending items to players

26.1.2JASM 0.6.3

Deck to Deck sends items to another player on your network. They travel for a while, then land in the other player's inbox.

How to send

  1. Pair your Deck at an Encoding Terminal.
  2. Press Deck to Deck on the Deck's screen and choose a player. Only players online on this network show up.
  3. Put the items in the send grid and press Send.

The grid is 1 slot on a Starter Deck, 3 on a Basic, 6 on an Advanced and 9 on Elite and Ultimate.

The trip

At a glance
Chargetwice what the same items would cost to move
Travel time5 seconds, plus about 1 second for every 64 blocks
Other dimension30 seconds more
Longest trip10 minutes
Trips at once10

Call back returns the items to your own inbox.

The inbox

Items land in the inbox. Store all moves them onto your Deck. If the inbox is full, the trip waits there until there is room.

Sending to another dimension needs a Dimension Upgrade on both Decks.
