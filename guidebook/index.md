JASM stores your items and fluids as data on small chips called wafers. Carry them in a handheld Deck, and back them up at an Archive, so a lost wafer can be rebuilt instead of gone for good.

## Using this guide

* Pages are listed in the sidebar. The magnifier at the top searches all of them.
* Hold the guide key (G by default) over an item's tooltip to jump to its page.
* Recipes on these pages are the real ones from the game.
