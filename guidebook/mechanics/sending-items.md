# Sending items to players

<ItemImage id="ultimate_deck" scale="4" />

Deck to Deck sends items to another player on your network. They travel for a while, then land in the other player's inbox.

<br />
<br />
## How to send

1. Pair your [Deck](../items/decks.md) at an [Encoding Terminal](../items/encoding-terminal.md).
2. Press **Deck to Deck** on the Deck's screen and choose a player. Only players online on this network show up.
3. Put the items in the send grid and press **Send**.

The grid is 1 slot on a Starter Deck, 3 on a Basic, 6 on an Advanced and 9 on Elite and Ultimate.

<br />
<br />
## The trip

| At a glance | |
| --- | --- |
| Charge | twice what the same items would cost to move |
| Travel time | 5 seconds, plus about 1 second for every 64 blocks |
| Other dimension | 30 seconds more |
| Longest trip | 3 minutes |
| Trips at once | 10 |

Changed your mind? **Call back** turns the trip round and brings the items to your own inbox.

<br />
<br />
## The inbox

Items land in the inbox. **Store all** moves them onto your Deck. If the inbox is full, the trip waits there until there is room.

Sending to another dimension needs a [Dimension Upgrade](../items/dimension-upgrade.md) on both Decks.
