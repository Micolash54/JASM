# Crafting rules

<ItemImage id="advanced_crafting_deck" scale="4" />

A rule makes the network craft for you, without you asking. Set them on the Rules tab of a [Crafting Deck](../items/crafting-decks.md).

## Two kinds

| Rule | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Fewer than | when I have fewer than 16 torches, craft 32 |
| Every | every minute, craft 8 bread |

Pick the item by holding it and clicking. A timer can be no shorter than 10 seconds by default.

## Where it goes

Choose where the results go: onto the Deck, or straight into your inventory. A "fewer than" rule counts what you carry when it sends to you.

## Good to know

* A rule runs one job at a time.
* If a job can't start, the rule waits 5 seconds and tries again. The rule's line says why.
* Each rule can be switched on or off.
* The rules need the same chain as [autocrafting](autocrafting.md): recipe cards, a rack, a server.
