# Getting started

Everything JASM stores lives on **wafers**. Put wafers in a **Deck** and carry it with you. Link the Deck to a powered **Archive** to keep backups, so a lost wafer can be rebuilt onto a blank one.

To open this guide at any time, craft the [JASM Guide](items/guide-book.md).
<br />
<br />
## A first hour

1. [Grow Data Crystals](mechanics/growing-crystals.md) on a block of amethyst.
2. [Cut them into chips](mechanics/making-chips.md) by hand.
3. Craft your first [Capacity Wafer](items/capacity-wafers.md) and a Starter [Deck](items/decks.md).
4. Build a [Chip Workshop](items/chip-workshop.md) and [befriend a wild Bitling](mechanics/befriending-bitlings.md) to work in it. Link Chips come only from there.

<br />
<br />
## How the tiers use the chips

The first tier of every wafer, Deck, generator and cable is made from vanilla items. From there on, recipes use JASM's own chips. Every higher tier needs the tier below it, and nothing it holds is lost on the way up.
