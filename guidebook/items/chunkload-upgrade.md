# Chunkload Upgrade

<ItemImage id="chunkload_upgrade" scale="4" />

Keeps the chunk its machine stands in loaded, even with nobody near. Put it in one of the machine's upgrade slots. A machine takes one.

<br />
<br />
## Machines that take it

* [Access Ports](access-port.md), thin or block.
* The [Crystal Foundry](crystal-foundry.md).
* The [Chip Workshop](chip-workshop.md).
* The [Deployment Bay](deployment-bay.md) and the [Demolition Bay](demolition-bay.md).

<br />
<br />
## Good to know

* It uses a little of the machine's power while it keeps a chunk loaded. The chunk is kept loaded only while the machine has power.
* With several in one chunk, the first one loads it and the rest wait. A waiting one takes over as soon as the one before it stops.
* The chunk stays loaded while you are away, and after a restart.
* Take the upgrade out and the machine's chunk is no longer kept loaded.
