# Speed Upgrade

<ItemImage id="speed_upgrade" scale="4" />

Makes a port, a bay, the Crystal Foundry or the Chip Workshop work faster.

<br />
<br />
## At a glance (Default values)

| Speed Upgrades | Speed | Power per job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | normal | normal |
| 1 | 2x | 1.25x |
| 2 | 4x | 1.5x |
| 3 | 8x | 2x |
| 4 | 16x | 2.5x |

* All of them have five upgrade slots, and a machine takes at most four Speed Upgrades. The other slots are for other upgrades, like the [Redstone Upgrade](redstone-upgrade.md).
* The Deployment and Demolition Bays, the Crystal Foundry and the [Chip Workshop](chip-workshop.md) share one rule: each upgrade doubles the speed, and every job costs a bit more power. In the Workshop this counts for chips and for the Synapse Core alike.
* A port with four of them moves 96 items every operation, up from 1. See [item ports](item-ports.md) and the [Access Port](access-port.md).
* Ports use no power themselves, with or without upgrades. The Deck they move items in and out of pays 1 FE for each item, as usual.

Upgrades stack normally in your inventory and in storage.
