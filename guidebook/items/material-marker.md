# Material

<ItemImage id="material_marker" scale="4" />

Stands for one of another mod's materials, a gas for example, on a [Recipe Card](recipe-cards.md). It is not a real item: you can't craft it or pick it up.

* In a [Network Encoder](encoding-terminal.md) set to Processing, right-click a slot with a container that holds the material. The slot then shows the material and its amount.
* The card needs that material: the job waits until the network's storage has it.
* A job's list shows the material by name, like any other ingredient.
