# Creative Battery

<BlockImage id="creative_battery" scale="8" />

Unlimited power, for creative worlds and testing. You can't craft it.

* It pushes up to 100,000 FE a tick out of each face.
* Its charging slot fills a [Deck](decks.md) or any other chargeable item at 100,000 FE a tick.
* It doesn't count toward a network's [machine limit](network-brain.md).
