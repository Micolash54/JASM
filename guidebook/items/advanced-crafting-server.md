# Advanced Crafting Server

<BlockImage id="advanced_crafting_server" scale="3.5" />

A [Crafting Server](crafting-server.md) two blocks tall, with room for ten of everything. It runs one job at a time like the small one, but with far more [Processors](processors.md) and [Storage Modules](storage-modules.md).

| At a glance | |
| --- | --- |
| Slots | 10 for Processors, 10 for Storage Modules |
| Uses | 40 FE a tick, plus its Processors |
| Counts toward | the [machine limit](network-brain.md) |

<br />
<br />
## Good to know

* Its front screen shows what the job makes and what is crafting right now.
* Its parts are locked in while it works.
* Breaking either half breaks both, and spills whatever its job held.
* Servers of both kinds can share a network. A job goes to one that fits and is free.
