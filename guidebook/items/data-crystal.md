# Data Crystal

<ItemImage id="data_crystal" scale="4" />

The Data Crystal is JASM's basic material. You grow it on <ItemLink id="seeded_amethyst" />.

* Mine a full cluster for Data Crystals, with your hand or any tool. Fortune gives more.
* Cut one into a <ItemLink id="blank_chip" /> at a Stonecutter.
* Four make a Wrench.
* One goes into a Synapse Core, which a Byteling builds in the Chip Workshop.
* Hand one to a wild Bitling and it follows you for about 30 seconds.
* With a book, it makes the <ItemLink id="guide_book" />.
